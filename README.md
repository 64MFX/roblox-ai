# roblox-ui-references
A curated collection of Roblox UI references for Claude Code, Codex, and other AI coding assistants to study and use as design inspiration

## Contents
- `studs-ui/`, `sports/`, `Icons/`, `Animation-Refs/`: image and video references
- [`ui-styles/`](ui-styles/GUI-STYLES.md): 112 GUI styles, one folder each (69 credited preview images in 30 folders), palettes, Roblox recipes and references
- [`animations/`](animations/ANIMATIONS.md): 33 UI and character/combat animation patterns (13 images in 9 folders)
- [`cutscenes/`](cutscenes/CUTSCENES.md): 18 camera, cinematic and dialogue patterns (4 images in 3 folders)
- [`vfx/`](vfx/VFX.md): 36 particle, beam, trail and feedback effects (28 images in 18 folders)

Preview images come from public itch.io listings and stay the property of their creators. See [ui-styles/IMAGE-CREDITS.md](ui-styles/IMAGE-CREDITS.md). Recipes are from general Roblox knowledge and linked DevForum threads and are not tested in Studio.
