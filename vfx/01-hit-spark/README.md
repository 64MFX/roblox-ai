# 1. Hit Spark

Bright burst at the point of impact.

**Roblox recipe (from general Roblox knowledge, not tested in Studio):** ParticleEmitter, Lifetime 0.15-0.3, Rate 0, call Emit(8-15), LightEmission 1.
